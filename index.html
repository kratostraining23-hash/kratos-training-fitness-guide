Ways to Hydrate Yourself

Proper hydration supports training and recovery. Here are practical ways to stay hydrated:

  • Drink fluids consistently through the day rather than trying to catch up all at once.
  • For long, intense, hot, or very sweaty sessions, consider an electrolyte drink and follow its label.
  • Eat water-rich foods such as watermelon, oranges, and cucumbers.
  • Carry a reusable water bottle to make drinking when thirsty convenient.
  • Sip during workouts and drink to thirst instead of forcing large amounts quickly.
  • Avoid excessive caffeine and alcohol, especially around training.
  • Use hydration products when they suit the workout; most short, easy sessions do not require them.
  • Add lemon or fruit to water if that makes it more enjoyable.

Sweat tests estimate fluid loss, not your individual sodium, potassium, or magnesium loss. Do not use this result to calculate mineral supplement doses. For medical conditions or medications affecting fluid or electrolytes, ask a healthcare professional for guidance.
